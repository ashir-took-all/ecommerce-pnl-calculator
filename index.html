Settlements & prepayments

Profit and cash flow can differ because inventory purchases, payment timing, COD settlements and other cash movements do not always occur at the same time as revenue and expenses.

The Inventory & COGS Rule

Zero Double-Counting
1
When you purchase inventory:

Recorded as a direct Cash Outflow on that day (e.g. −Rs 300,000). Cash leaves your bank immediately.

2
When items are sold later:

Recorded as COGS on your P&L statement to determine profit. Cash Flow does NOT subtract COGS again, preserving true liquidity.
